# Ideas for Later

Things asked for that belong somewhere other than the everyday pages.

## A Reports page

- **Projections vs. reality:** what each month's projection said on each day against how the month
  ended, so it's clear how good the estimates are and where they miss.
- **Staying within budget:** month by month, which budgets held and which ran over, and by how much.
- **Where each budget is spent:** each budget's usual share per card (the "91% usually on this card"
  detail taken off the Budget page).
